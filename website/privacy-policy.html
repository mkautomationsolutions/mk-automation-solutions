Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how MK Automation Solutions collects, uses, stores and protects information through its website, AgencyOS platform, ReviewBoost AI and related automation services.

Effective date: 2 August 2026 · Contact: mkautomationsol.ai@gmail.com

1. About MK Automation Solutions

MK Automation Solutions provides business automation, customer communication, review collection, lead management, service follow-up and related software services. Our products may include AgencyOS, ReviewBoost AI and other modules that are made available to businesses and their authorised users.

2. Information we collect

The information we collect depends on how you interact with our website and services.

Website and enquiry information

When you submit an enquiry or contact form, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, business or company name, selected service and the message you provide.

AgencyOS account information

When a business or authorised user uses AgencyOS, we may process account information such as name, email address, phone number, business name, organisation membership, branch, role, permissions, authentication records and account status.

Customer and service information

Businesses using our services may upload or enter information about their customers. This may include a customer name, mobile number, language preference, service or transaction description, appointment or follow-up information and other information required to provide the requested automation.

ReviewBoost and feedback information

ReviewBoost AI may process review-request records, customer feedback, star ratings, private comments, review links, public request tokens, response status and the date and time of relevant activity.

WhatsApp communication information

Where WhatsApp automation is enabled, we may process WhatsApp Business Account identifiers, sender phone number identifiers, recipient mobile numbers, approved message-template information, message IDs, delivery events and statuses such as scheduled, processing, sent, delivered, read or failed.

Depending on the product and configuration, we may also process information necessary to create message links, schedule reminders, prevent duplicate sends, retry failed jobs and maintain communication history.

Usage and billing information

We may process subscription plan information, included usage limits, billable units, sent or delivered counts, overage information, payment status, setup-fee status, billing period and access restrictions.

Technical and security information

We may automatically receive technical information such as timestamps, IP address, browser or device information, request logs, error details, workflow execution data and security events.

3. How we use information

We may use information to:

  • provide and operate our website and software;
  • create and manage business, team and customer accounts;
  • send authorised review requests, reminders, follow-ups and notifications;
  • process customer feedback and service responses;
  • track message delivery, failures, usage and system performance;
  • enforce subscription plans, permissions and usage limits;
  • provide customer support and troubleshoot errors;
  • maintain operational, security and audit records;
  • prevent fraud, abuse, duplicate messaging and unauthorised access;
  • improve our products, workflows and services; and
  • comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

4. Business-customer responsibilities

A business using AgencyOS or ReviewBoost AI is responsible for ensuring that it has a lawful reason and any required consent or permission to collect, upload and use its customers’ information and to contact those customers through WhatsApp, email, telephone or other communication channels.

Businesses must not use our services to send unlawful, misleading, abusive, unsolicited or prohibited communications. They are also responsible for complying with applicable WhatsApp and Meta policies.

Where we process customer information on behalf of a business, we generally act according to that business’s authorised instructions and the enabled product configuration.

5. Service providers and integrations

We may use third-party infrastructure and service providers to operate our products. Depending on the enabled features, these providers may include:

  • Meta and the WhatsApp Business Platform;
  • database and authentication providers;
  • workflow-automation platforms;
  • cloud hosting and deployment providers;
  • email-delivery providers;
  • payment and billing providers; and
  • monitoring, security and support providers.

These providers may process information only as necessary to provide their services, subject to their applicable terms, privacy policies and contractual obligations.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information.

We may share or make information available:

  • to the business that controls the relevant AgencyOS account;
  • to authorised owners, administrators and team members of that business;
  • to service providers supporting our platform;
  • when required to investigate misuse, protect rights or maintain platform security;
  • when required by law, legal process or a competent authority; or
  • in connection with a merger, acquisition or transfer of business assets, subject to appropriate safeguards.

7. Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain business and security records, process billing, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations and protect our systems.

Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information, the applicable product, the business customer’s instructions and legal or operational requirements.

Information may be deleted, anonymised or aggregated when it is no longer required. Some records may be retained when necessary for security, fraud prevention, accounting, dispute resolution or legal compliance.

8. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or loss.

These measures may include role-based access, authentication controls, restricted service credentials, encrypted network connections, database security policies, activity logging and backup procedures.

No online system can guarantee absolute security. Users must protect their login credentials and must not share passwords, access tokens, API keys or other confidential credentials through public forms or messages.

9. Cookies and analytics

Our website and applications may use essential storage technologies required for authentication, security, session management and application operation.

If we introduce analytics, advertising cookies or other non-essential tracking technologies, we may update this policy and provide any choices required by applicable law.
