Frequently asked questions

What businesses usually ask before starting.

Every project is different, but these answers explain the way we approach automation work.

What can you automate?

Lead capture, follow-ups, email notifications, approval flows, CRM updates, reporting, data synchronisation, customer support and other structured repetitive processes.

Can you connect our existing tools?

Yes. We can work with webhooks, APIs, databases and supported integrations. The exact approach depends on the access and capabilities of each tool.

Do you build custom CRM systems?

Yes. We can build focused CRM workflows for leads, assignments, follow-ups, team access, analytics and reporting instead of forcing every business into the same generic process.

Will every AI tool need a separate login?

No. The planned customer app will use one account and one login, with tools enabled according to the customer plan and workspace permissions.

How will the WhatsApp follow-up tool be used?

It is planned for opted-in leads, approved message templates, reply tracking and human hand-off—not unsolicited bulk messaging.

How long does an automation project take?

A small workflow may take a few days, while a multi-module CRM or automation platform takes longer. The timeline is confirmed after requirements and dependencies are mapped.

Will our team receive support after launch?

Handover, documentation and post-launch support can be included in the project plan. Ongoing monitoring or improvement support can also be scoped separately.
